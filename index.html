How to get a transcript fast

YouTube: under the video, open …more then Show transcript, and copy it.

TikTok / Reels: most short videos have captions burned in. Save the video (or screen-record it) and upload the file above. Claude reads the captions frame by frame.

Word-for-word audio transcript: run python tools/transcribe.py <link> from this project's repo. It downloads the audio and runs Whisper on your computer. Paste what it prints into the box above.
